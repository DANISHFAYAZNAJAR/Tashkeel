The Problem We Solve
Over a Billion Muslims
Cannot Understand
Their Own Prayers

Arabic is the language of the Quran — but less than 20% of the world's Muslims speak it. Billions attend prayers, sermons, and lectures every week without understanding what is being said.

❌

Spiritual Disconnection

Non-Arabic speakers cannot engage deeply with the meaning of prayers, reducing the spiritual impact of Salah, Taraweeh, and Jumu'ah.

❌

Language Barrier in the Ummah

Mosques in non-Arab countries serve diverse communities — Urdu, Bengali, English, French speakers — but there has been no technology solution until now.

✅

Tashkeel Bridges the Gap

Real-time AI translation, delivered silently and privately into each person's ear. The spiritual atmosphere is preserved. The understanding is unlocked.

How It Works
Four Steps.
Complete Understanding.
🎙️

Imam Speaks Naturally

A small, high-quality mic is placed at the pulpit or worn as a lapel. No change to how the Imam conducts prayers or lectures.

🧠

AI Translates in Real Time

Advanced speech recognition identifies Arabic, Urdu, or any language — and translates it into the worshipper's selected language within under one second.

📡

Wireless Broadcast

Translated audio is broadcast securely within the mosque over a low-latency wireless network. People can also connect via a mobile app by scanning a QR code.

🎧

Personal Earpiece

Each worshipper wears a discreet, comfortable earpiece pre-set to their preferred language. They hear translations privately, without disturbing others.

🕌

Spiritually Respectful

Completely silent and invisible to others. The atmosphere of prayer is not disturbed. Translation happens privately in the ear — not on screens, not via speakers.

Mosques can buy a set of earpieces for communal use, or individuals can purchase their own and link to any mosque's channel via a QR code using the Tashkeel app.

Our AI processing pipeline is optimised for real-time speech, delivering translation with near-zero perceptible delay — so you hear the meaning as the Imam speaks.
